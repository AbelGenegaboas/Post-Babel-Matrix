# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, oriented toward *Ad Maiorem Dei Gloriam* (For the Greater Glory of God).

## Our Standards
This repository bridges deep Catholic theology with systems architecture. We expect rigorous, uncompromising academic and structural debate. We do not tolerate:
* Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
* Disrespect toward the theological premises foundational to this project.
* Publishing others' private information, such as physical or electronic addresses.
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic setting.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, or to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for other behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers directly.
